chadtab SPEC -- what it must do.  tests/run.sh checks each line (its number is in the failure).
Copyright 2026 INPUTLOOP (Bryant Frankford and Corwin Brust); CC-BY-SA-4.0.  Mentat (for Corwin; claude-opus-5-5).

1  A clock's next fires match the hand-worked rows of tests/VECTORS.tsv (counted on a calendar, not by either
   reader), in both readers: chadtab (Python) and chadtab.el (Emacs Lisp).
2  On the "agree" rows the two readers give the same fires.  They share no code: the Python one walks minutes and
   skips ahead; the Lisp one walks days, then the hours and minutes the spec allows.
3  every: is aligned to the epoch in UTC, so the same row in different zones gives the fires of tests/VECTORS-TZ.tsv.
4  A table reads into settings, a run-level, and blocks with their clocks, levels and catchup; a malformed line
   (a four-field cron, a step before any block, a step with no arrow, a second block of one name) is refused
   with exit 2, naming its line where it has one.
5  A run takes a command's last output line apart by the chadgram rules (the ..field keeps the rest), appends its
   record as one JSON line, and binds a value into a registry with both writers (r and c), which then verifies.
6  A guarded write advances the semaphore to its own number; the c side drops the old value; a reader at a level
   at or under the writer's sees it, one above does not; nobody sees past the semaphore.
7  When the two writers disagree, the write is refused (exit 4), the old value is put back on both sides, the
   journal says FAILED, and the semaphore does not move.
8  A held lock keeps a second writer out (exit 5) and nothing is written.
9  The naming rule is cxr's: chadtab enc and tv1cr enc agree on every case (a separate sh implementation).
10 The daemon, ticked at a set time, runs what is due and nothing else; a boot block runs on boot; a catchup
   block runs once for a fire missed while it was down; a block above the daemon's level does not run; a minute
   later the same block does not run again.
11 status --json carries each block's last result, a history strip, its next day of fires (at most 96) and the registries it binds; the text view lists every block.
Planted faults (--prove-red), each must turn the kit red: domor everylocal nolock onewriter noguard nosem levelinv.
